Webtoon Panel Cutter

Checking current mode...

Cutter: right-click pairs of horizontal lines, then End & Save.

Box: right-click and drag to mark rectangles, then End & Save.

Section: right-click marks the visible viewport, then End & Save.

Use Ctrl+Z to remove the last item placed.

Panels are saved automatically to your Downloads folder inside a subfolder named mode-YYYYMMDD-HHmmss.

Tip: turn off “Ask where to save each file” in Chrome settings for uninterrupted saving.
